Matte effect Lightroom presets are perfect for creating soft, cinematic edits with a timeless and slightly faded aesthetic.

The matte look is created by lifting the darker tones in an image, reducing harsh contrast, and softening colors to produce a smoother, more atmospheric feel.

This editing style became especially popular in film-inspired photography and continues to be widely used in portrait, lifestyle, travel, and Instagram photography today.

Below, you’ll discover a curated collection of the best matte effect Lightroom presets.

These presets are perfect for creating elegant, film-inspired edits with soft tones and a cohesive visual style.
