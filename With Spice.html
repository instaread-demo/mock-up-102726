The negroni sbagliato is a spritzier take on the classic Negroni, made with Campari, sweet vermouth, and Prosecco.

Why I love it:

A Negroni Sbagliato is a lighter, spritzier take on the classic Negroni. Instead of gin, it's topped with Prosecco, which softens the bitterness and adds bright bubbles without losing that unmistakable Campari character.

The first time I had one was while I was living in Italy, and it quickly became one of my favorite aperitivo cocktails. It has all the herbal, bittersweet complexity of a Negroni, but it feels brighter, more refreshing, and a little easier to sip before dinner.

I think of it as somewhere between a Negroni and a Campari Spritz. The sweet vermouth adds rich, dark fruit notes while the sparkling wine keeps everything light and lively. It's simple, elegant, and exactly the kind of drink I want with a bowl of olives, potato chips, or a platter of Italian cheeses and cured meats.

Recipe Tips

Use a dry Prosecco. Brut or Extra Dry Prosecco balances the sweetness of the vermouth and Campari without making the cocktail cloying.

Serve over plenty of ice. A full glass of ice keeps the drink cold while slowing dilution.

Express the orange peel. If using a peel instead of a wedge, squeeze it over the drink before dropping it into the glass. The citrus oils add a wonderful aroma with every sip.

The Negroni Sbagliato is a spritzier take on the classic Negroni, made with Campari, sweet vermouth, and Prosecco.

  • Author: Indi Hampton
  • Prep Time: 5 minutes
  • Total Time: 5 minutes
  • Yield: 1
  • Category: Cocktail
  • Method: Stirred
  • Cuisine: Italian

Ingredients

  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 1 oz Prosecco or dry sparkling wine
  • Orange peel or wedge, for serving

Instructions

  1. Fill a rocks glass ice.
  2. Add Campari and vermouth and stir to combine.
  3. Top with Prosecco and stir again. Garnish with an orange peel or wedge, if desired.
