Co-Defendant Rejects Wired Plea Deal 3 Months Before Homicide Trial

October 9 — Sophia Cooper

A co-defendant in a 2024 fatal shooting case rejected a wired plea deal before DC Superior Court Judge Todd Edelman on Oct. 9. 

Karim Ibrahim, 23, and Delonte Terrell, 30, are charged with first-degree premeditated murder while armed, three counts of assault with intent to kill while armed, and assault with significant bodily injury while armed. Ibrahim is also charged with five counts of possession of a firearm during a crime of violence, and carrying a pistol without a license outside a home or business.

The charges stem from their alleged involvement in the fatal shooting of 23-year-old Javonni Coleman on Jan. 16, 2024 on the 2400 block of S Street, SE. Coleman sustained gunshot wounds to his head, torso, and lower extremities.

Ibrahim, with his attorney Kevin Mosley, was the only defendant present at the hearing.

The prosecutor said they offered Ibrahim a plea deal that was wired to Terrell, contingent upon both co-defendants’ acceptance. If Ibrahim pleaded guilty to second-degree murder while armed, the prosecution would dismiss all greater and remaining charges against him. The prosecution would reserve the right to request a sentence within Ibrahim’s applicable guideline range.

Judge Edelman confirmed that Ibrahim rejected the offer and wanted to proceed to trial. Both co-defendants are scheduled to start their trial on Jan. 19, 2027.

The prosecutor did not specify the terms of the wired plea for Terrell.

Judge Edelman also addressed an Oct. 7 notice of Ibrahim’s non-compliance with release conditions for a 15-minute curfew violation. The judge noted that it was a minor violation, but reminded Ibrahim of his obligation to maintain the curfew.

The parties for both Ibrahim and Terrell are scheduled to reconvene on Nov. 20.
