Cue the confetti, because we don’t have to turn on the oven for this one!

If you’re a fan of that classic peanut butter and chocolate flavor combo (like me!), you’re in for a treat. These easy, no-bake peanut butter bars taste *just like* Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups!

This chocolate peanut butter bar features a rich, melt-in-your-mouth peanut butter cookie base (made with graham cracker crumbs) that’s covered with a glossy layer of chocolate over the top. They have a perfect sweet-salty crunch that is the stuff of dreams.

For jazz hands, I added mini chocolate chips to the cookie bar base and roasted salted peanuts to the tops. Both are optional but highly recommended!

Second to the incredible flavor is the unbelievable ease with which these bars are thrown together. You’ll use one bowl to mix; there’s no baking or hot oven to deal with and you just pop them in the fridge and they’re ready for snacking within a few hours! They’re the perfect grab-and-go snack!

Imagine a world where minimal effort yields decadent snacking…yes, this is that world!

I hope you love these as much as we do; my dad has already asked for a second batch!

My Favorite Tips For
The Best No-Bake Chocolate Peanut Butter Bars!

  1. Grind the graham crackers until quite fine. Just like making a graham cracker crust for your cheesecake, you want the graham crackers to be quite fine so that they form a cohesive ‘dough’. If there are larger chunks of graham crackers, it makes it harder for it to form a firm cookie bar base.
  2. Use the right kind of peanut butter. Like most peanut butter baking (and no-bake) recipes, these benefit from smooth (processed) peanut butter. I love Jiff or Skippy for these bars. Natural peanut butters are less consistent in their texture (some are super thick or super runny), which can impact the final cookie consistency.
  3. These no-bake cookie bars are best stored in the fridge (in my opinion!). They make the perfect grab-and-go snack and are delicious (refreshing?) when eaten chilled.
  4. Use a sharp knife to cut the bars. For nice, clean cuts, run a sharp knife under warm water and dry completely. Press down gently to cut. If you cut too fast, they might crack or crumble.

Ultimate No-Bake Chocolate Peanut Butter Bars

These mega-easy bars feature a rich peanut butter cookie base that's topped with a glossy layer of chocolate and chilled to perfection! They taste just like a homemade Reese's Peanut Butter Cup and take only about 15 minutes to make. Perfect for BBQs, late-night snacking or whenever those intense chocolate-peanut butter cravings hit!

Prep Time: 15 minutes minutes
Course: Dessert, Snack
Keyword: No-Bake Chocolate Peanut Butter Bars
Servings: 16
Author: Rebecca Firth

Equipment

8-inch (20-cm) Square Baking Pan
Chef's Knife
Mixing Bowls
Baking Scale

Ingredients

For the Peanut Butter Cookie Base

  • 1 cup (256 g) smooth peanut butter (see Notes below the recipe)
  • 8 tablespoons (113 g) unsalted butter (melted)
  • 1 tablespoon real vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups (180 g) confectioners’ sugar (also called powdered sugar, icing sugar or 10x)
  • ¼ cup (55 g) light or dark brown sugar (packed)
  • 1 cup (130 g) graham crackers (crushed until fine (about 8 graham crackers))
  • 1/3 cup (57 g) mini chocolate chips (optional)

For the Chocolate Topping

  • 1 ¼ cup (213 g) dark chocolate (finely chopped)
  • 4 tablespoons (64 g) smooth peanut butter

To Garnish (optional)

  • ½ cup (57 g) roasted salted peanuts
  • sea salt flakes

Instructions

Prep Your Pan

  • Grease an 8×8-inch (20×20-cm) or 9×9-inch (23×23-cm) baking pan and line with parchment paper, letting the excess hang over the sides of the pan. This will make it easier to get the bars out of the pan later. Set aside.
    • Using an 8×8-inch (20×20-cm) baking pan will yield thicker bars, while a 9×9-inch (23×23-cm) will give you thinner peanut butter bars.

Mix The Peanut Butter Cookie Bar Base

  • In a medium, heat-safe bowl using a silicone spatula, mix the peanut butter, melted butter and vanilla until smooth. Stir in the confectioners’ sugar, brown sugar and finely ground graham cracker crumbs until well combined. Finally, mix in the mini chocolate chips, if using, until evenly distributed throughout the dough.
  • Scoop into the prepared pan and press evenly until you have a nice, cohesive cookie base.
    • Rinse and dry the mixing bowl clean of any crumbs and use it for the next step.

Melt The Chocolate Topping & Assemble The Cookie Bars

  • For the chocolate topping, add the chocolate and peanut butter to the medium, heat-safe bowl and set over a medium saucepan of simmering water. Do not let the bowl touch the water or let the water come to a boil. Stir frequently until melted and smooth and then pour over the top of the peanut butter cookie base. You can either move the pan around to get the chocolate to cover the top completely or use an offset spatula or the back of a spoon to smooth the top of the cookies.
  • If using, immediately sprinkle with roasted salted peanuts and/or sea salt flakes and set the pan in the refrigerator for several hours for the cookies to set.

To Serve

  • I like these served best while slightly chilled. When ready to cut the bars, let them sit on the counter for 10 minutes. Run a sharp knife under warm water, dry it completely and then cut the cookie bars. This will help you get the cleanest cuts of the bars.
  • Store the bars in an airtight container in the fridge for up to one week and enjoy!

Notes

  • Peanut Butter
    • Most peanut butter cookie recipes call for using smooth peanut butter such as Jiff and Skippy. This is because the consistency is so, well, consistent.
    • This recipe was tested using both of those brands, as well as natural peanut butter (I love the Santa Cruz brand!).
    • If you use the more-processed peanut butter you are guaranteed the perfect texture in the cookie. If you choose a natural peanut butter (that’s typically just peanuts and salt), just make sure it has a similar consistency as the processed brands. If you use natural peanut butter make sure you stir it really well before using.
    • The ideal peanut butter has zero or minimal sugar added. 
    • The peanut butter you choose will impact how the cookie looks and tastes. If you want the exact look as the cookies in the photos then use either a Jiff or Skippy-type smooth peanut butter.
  • Chocolate
    • For the chocolate topping, try to use chocolate that is made for melting, such as baking chocolate, feves or wafers. Chocolate bars are another great option. You can use chocolate chips in a pinch, but they have ingredients in them that make them resistant to melting and might not yield the perfectly smooth, glossy top.
    • I don’t recommend milk chocolate for the topping as it would be too sweet, in my opinion.
  • Graham Crackers
    • You want the graham crackers very finely ground for best results. You can do this by placing them in a resealable plastic bag and using a rolling pin to gently crush them. You can also use a food processor fitted with the blade attachment or a high-speed blender.
    • You can substitute the graham crackers with Biscoff cookies or vanilla wafers. I also love them made with finely ground pretzels for a less-sweet treat!
